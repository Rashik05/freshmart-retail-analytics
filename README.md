## Project Overview

FreshMart Retail Analytics is an end-to-end business intelligence project inspired by real retail operations. The project combines SQL, MySQL, Power BI, DAX, and Power Query to transform raw retail data into interactive dashboards that support sales analysis, inventory planning, supplier evaluation, and business decision-making.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of sales, inventory, purchasing, and supplier transactions every day. Without centralized reporting, it becomes difficult to monitor performance, identify trends, optimize inventory, and make informed business decisions.

This project was developed to build a complete business intelligence solution that transforms raw retail data into meaningful insights through SQL analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards.

## Dataset

The project includes a relational retail dataset consisting of:

- Product Master
- Sales Fact
- Purchase Fact
- Inventory Fact
- Supplier Master
- Categories
- Date Dimension

The data was prepared and validated before being imported into MySQL for analysis and Power BI for visualization.

## Business Insights

The dashboards help answer questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories are most profitable?
- Which products are below their reorder level?
- Which suppliers contribute the most revenue?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which products are slow moving?

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Prioritize replenishment for products below reorder levels.
- Reduce slow-moving inventory to improve cash flow.
- Invest in high-performing product categories.
- Monitor supplier performance regularly.
- Promote high-margin products.
- Track KPIs through the executive dashboard.
